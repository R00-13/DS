About us

A school built on technology.

Digital School is a technology school for children and young people aged 8–18, focused on programming and digital skills.

Our motto

Improvise. Code. Overcome.

Our vision is to equip children and young people with the skills they need to succeed in a world where technology and digital literacy are part of every profession.

A school that builds its own tools, too.

About the school

Who we are

Who we are

A specialized technology education school, with a structured program, regular assessment, and a focus on every student's progress.

What we've built

A curriculum, platforms and systems developed in-house at Digital School — plus instructors trained in the same methodology.

What we offer

In-person or online learning. Through programming, students build digital skills, critical thinking, creativity and problem-solving.

Beyond the classroom

Learning continues through projects, competitions, community and the wider Digital School ecosystem.

Our presence

Where we operate

Our students are part of an international Digital School community, with centers in different countries around the world. Digital School Macedonia is part of the global Digital School network.

Founders

Co-founders of Digital School Macedonia

AA
Co-Founder & CEO

Arjeta Aliu

Arjeta built her career at the intersection of education, management and people development. She holds a Master's degree from the Faculty of Technology and Metallurgy at Ss. Cyril and Methodius University in Skopje and is an engineer in Biochemistry, bringing over 25 years of experience in formal education and more than 20 years in non-formal education. Since 2004 she has led private education institutions. At Digital School Macedonia, she leads with the belief that education shouldn't just prepare young people for the future — it should give them the courage and the ability to create it.

AA
Co-Founder & CTO

Azir Aliu

Azir is a university professor and holds a PhD in Computer Science. As Co-Founder and CTO, he is responsible for the curriculum, innovation and technology direction of the academy. With extensive experience in higher education, research, artificial intelligence and digital transformation, he also mentors startups and has designed innovative programs and strategies at national level. He holds a Digital Transformation certification from MIT Professional Education, USA. At Digital School Macedonia, he leads program development with one clear idea: children shouldn't grow up only as users of technology, but as its creators — able to think, experiment, and build what doesn't exist yet.
